# Overview

**Duels without risk:** two players fight in their own arena with a given kit - and afterwards get **everything back exactly as it was**. With their own statistics, a leaderboard and protection against stat farming.

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 26.3 (or a fork like Purpur), Java 25 |
| Requires | nothing - MRadmin optional |
| Languages | German, English - automatically like the Minecraft client |
| Status | pre-release (0.x) - config, menus and permissions may still change until 1.0.0 |

## What can MRpvp do?
- **Challenge via menu:** choose player, kit, arena and rules, send the invitation - the opponent accepts with a click in chat. → [How a duel works](duell.md)
- **Nothing gets lost:** inventory, armor, ender chest, XP, health, hunger, effects, game mode and position are saved safely **before** the duel and given back afterwards - even after a server crash.
- **Duels do not count for Minecraft:** no deaths and kills in the vanilla statistics, no death message, no drops, no advancements.
- **14 ready-made kits** (Classic, Netherite, UHC, Sumo, Boxing, Crystal, Elytra & Mace …) and **4 arenas** - you build your own in game. → [Kits](kits.md), [Arenas](arenen.md)
- **Own statistics:** K/D, wins, streaks, leaderboard and the last rounds with a combat log. → [Stats & leaderboard](statistik.md)
- **Fair scoring:** suspicious rounds (e.g. arranged stat farming) wait for a review by the team. → [Reviewing suspicious rounds](pruefen.md)
- **Everything via menus** - for players and for admins.
