# Stats & leaderboard

In the duel menu (`/duel`) every player sees:
- their **statistics** - overall and per kit (K/D, wins, win rate, streaks),
- the **leaderboard** - wins, K/D, win rate, best streak; overall, 30 or 7 days,
- their **last rounds** with a combat log.

Every round has a **game ID** (e.g. `#1042`) the team can find it by - mention it if you have questions about a round.

## Why does a round not count?
It was marked as suspicious - e.g. too many rounds against the same opponent or a very short round without hits - and waits for a review by the team. After 7 days at the latest it is decided automatically. Balanced rounds among friends are never suspicious.

## In the browser
With MRadmin you also see your duel statistics, the last rounds and the duel rankings on your player page - `/web` in game gives you the link.
